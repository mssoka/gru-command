**Always:** Bind head/base/diff, spec, accessible tracked assets/verification evidence, policy/tool/runtime/model and job/round owner lineage. Compare content across rounds without equating round IDs; validate write-once source bytes. Keep rejected outputs only as evidence. Reconcile old children before replacement; uncertain ownership blocks. Preserve every started attempt (even failed evidence writes), the 16-run round cap and two-attempt/lens cap. **Never:** Inherit clearance, verdicts or specialist credit under changed/unknown identity; treat zero findings or a report as READY; refund attempts or bypass isolation, pacing or cancellation. No resource-policy edits, merge, restart or destructive ledger repairs.
